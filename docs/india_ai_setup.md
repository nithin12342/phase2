# IndiaAI Compute Portal - Student Setup Guide

## 1. What is IndiaAI Compute?
The **IndiaAI Compute Portal** provides specialized GPU resources (NVIDIA A100/H100) to students, researchers, and startups.
- **Benefit:** Access to high-performance compute for training complex models like H5-OmniFusion.
- **Cost:** Subsidized or free for eligible academic projects.

## 2. Prerequisites
Before applying, you must have:
- **Active Student Status:** Undergraduate, Postgraduate, or PhD.
- **APAAR ID:** Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry ID.

## 3. How to Obtain an APAAR ID
The APAAR ID is mandatory for the application. You can generate it via **DigiLocker** or the **ABC Portal**.

### **Method A: Via DigiLocker (Fastest)**
1.  **Login:** Go to [DigiLocker](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) or use the app.
2.  **Search:** Click on "Search Documents" and type **"APAAR"** or **"ABC ID"**.
3.  **Select:** Choose **"APAAR/ABC ID Card"** (Academic Bank of Credits).
4.  **Enter Details:**
    *   **Institution Type:** University/College/Skill.
    *   **Institution Name:** Select your college.
    *   **Identity Type:** Roll Number, Registration Number, or Enrollment Number.
    *   **Identity Value:** Enter the corresponding number.
5.  **Generate:** Click "Get Document". Your 12-digit APAAR ID card will be generated instantly.

### **Method B: Via ABC Portal**
1.  Visit [abc.gov.in](https://www.abc.gov.in/).
2.  Click **My Account > Student**.
3.  Login via **DigiLocker** (using Mobile/Aadhaar).
4.  If prompted, enter your University details to generate the ID.

## 4. Troubleshooting: Forgot DigiLocker PIN / DOB

If you forgot your PIN or Date of Birth (DOB) for DigiLocker, follow these steps to recover access:

### **Step 1: Get Your Date of Birth (DOB)**
You **must** know the exact DOB registered with your Aadhaar to reset the PIN.
*   **Check Physical Card:** Look at your physical Aadhaar card.
*   **Check e-Aadhaar:** If you don't have the card, download your e-Aadhaar from [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) using your mobile number (OTP required). The PDF password is the first 4 letters of your name (CAPS) + Year of Birth (YYYY).

### **Step 2: Reset Security PIN**
1.  Go to the DigiLocker **Login** page.
2.  Click **Forgot Security PIN?**.
3.  Enter your **Aadhaar Number** and the **Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY)** you found in Step 1.
4.  Click **Next**.
5.  Enter the **OTP** sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile number.
6.  Set a **New 6-digit PIN**.

You can now log in and generate your APAAR ID.

---

## 5. Applying for Compute Resources
Once you have your APAAR ID, follow these steps to apply for the H5-OmniFusion project:

1.  **Register:** Go to the [IndiaAI Compute Portal](https://indiaai.gov.in/) (or specific compute subdomain) and login via **Meri Pehchaan**.
2.  **Profile:** Complete your profile using your **APAAR ID** and **Institutional Email**.
3.  **Project Proposal:** You will need to submit a proposal.
    *   **Title:** *H5-OmniFusion: Multimodal Depression Detection with RL-Based Optimization*
    *   **Abstract:** (Use the abstract from your research report)
    *   **Compute Need:** Specify "High-Performance GPU (A100/H100)" for "Multimodal Transformer Training & RL Fine-tuning".
4.  **Documents:**
    *   Upload **Student ID** / **Enrollment Proof**.
    *   Upload **Endorsement Letter** from your Project Guide (Professor).

> [!TIP]
> **Request Strategy:** Start with a request under **5,000 GPU hours** if possible, as these are often auto-approved or fast-tracked.
